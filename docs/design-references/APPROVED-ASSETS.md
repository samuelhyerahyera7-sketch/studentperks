# Approved UI asset map

These files come directly from the approved September 7 mockups. They are the visual source of truth.

## Rules for implementation

1. Do not generate replacement photography, logos, icons, wording, or decorative marks.
2. Use the files under `assets/ui-approved/` for the exact artwork shown in the mockups.
3. Use the individual SVG files under `assets/icons/` and `assets/brand/` for interface icons and decorative strokes.
4. Keep all headings, labels, and buttons as live HTML text unless the text is intentionally baked into one of the approved artwork crops.
5. Match the approved mobile reference at 706 px wide before adapting it responsively.
6. The two uploaded business-dashboard screenshots are identical; only one canonical reference is required.

## SVGs

The SVGs under `assets/icons/` and `assets/brand/` are reusable implementation assets. The raster crops above are exact pieces of the approved mockups; the SVGs are manually reconstructed vectors and should not be mistaken for recoverable source layers from the generated mockup.

## Important production note

The approved images were generated as flattened mockups. Their internal photographs and partner logos were not delivered as separate original source files. The crops preserve the approved appearance exactly, but official partner-provided brand artwork should replace generated brand imagery before production launch.
