Help
How do students redeem my offer?
They show their StudentPerks code at checkout, or your staff enter it under Overview's "Check a code" — reachable any time from your account manager's instructions. Marking it redeemed updates this dashboard immediately.
Why don't I see "offer views" or store locations?
Those aren't tracked yet — this dashboard only shows numbers backed by real data (codes issued, claimed and redeemed). Ask your account manager if you'd like view-tracking or multi-branch reporting added.
