Verification Workflow
1
Application arrives by email
FormSubmit sends you a table-formatted email at deniloans6@gmail.com with the student's full name, personal email, student email, institution, year, degree, student number, and their student card photo attachment.
2
Review the student card
Open the attached photo. Confirm the name matches what they entered, the institution matches their selection, and the card shows a current enrolment year. Check the student number if visible.
3
Send an approval or rejection email
Reply to the applicant's personal email. For approvals, welcome them and explain how to access deals (for now, direct them to the site). For rejections, let them know what was missing — they can reapply.
4
Log the decision below
Use "Add approval entry" to record the outcome. This keeps a local browser log of decisions so you don't lose track. Use "Export log (CSV)" to save a backup at any time.
