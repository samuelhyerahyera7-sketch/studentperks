Verification workflow
1. Student submits application → appears in Applications tab.
2. Review photo + details → Approve or Reject (add a note).
3. When approving, go to Coupons → assign codes to the student's email.
4. Email the student their codes — they redeem at studentperks.co.za/redeem.
5. Vendors verify codes at studentperks.co.za/verify using their PIN.
